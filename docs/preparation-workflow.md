# Preparation Workflow

This module covers the planning and feedback loop for student preparation.

## Flow

1. Collect student profile and target role.
2. Compare role requirements with current skills and gaps.
3. Rank preparation topics by job importance, weakness, and available time.
4. Generate a daily study plan.
5. Launch the relevant assessment type.
6. Evaluate answers and compute score, time, and weak topics.
7. Summarize historical performance and suggest re-assessment.

## Key decisions

- Topic priority is driven by role relevance and performance gaps.
- Daily plans remain brief and realistic for the chosen time budget.
- Assessment logic intentionally avoids exposing answers before submission.
- Performance analysis blends accuracy, topic importance, and recent trend.
